# Responsible use

AgentShield attack modules exist for defensive evaluation of local demos, systems you own, or systems you are explicitly authorized to assess. Keep targets controlled, simulated, and least-privileged. Do not use the corpus to access third-party systems, evade controls, obtain credentials, or cause real side effects.

Researchers should minimize sensitive data, disclose vulnerabilities privately, distinguish modeled limitations from implementation flaws, and follow applicable law and institutional rules. AgentShield is not offensive automation.
